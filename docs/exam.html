Instructions

  1. The timer starts when you press Start test. When it reaches zero, the test submits automatically.
  2. Click an option to choose it. Answers save automatically, online and on this device.
  3. Save & Next moves to the next question. Mark for Review & Next flags the question so you can come back to it.
  4. Questions that are answered and marked for review are still counted when scored.
  5. Jump to any question from the palette on the right.
  6. Closing the tab is safe. Log in on any device and open the paper to resume. The timer keeps running while you are away.
  7. Keyboard: A-E or 1-5 choose an option, → next, ← previous, M mark for review.
